How we treat the island

The island is the hero, not a backdrop. Six rules every frame follows.

  1. Real places only

    Every setting exists on the island and is matched to a recce reference. Nothing invented.

  2. Architecture kept true

    Towers, bridges, promenade and materials shown as they are built.

  3. Sightlines that add up

    What is behind the cast is what you would really see from that spot.

  4. Light true to the hour

    Sun, shadows and reflections consistent with the time and the view.

  5. No third-party brands

    Tenant logos, signage and AI text removed, unless you clear them.

  6. At its best, never altered

    Tidied like a hero shoot: no clutter or construction. Never reshaped.
